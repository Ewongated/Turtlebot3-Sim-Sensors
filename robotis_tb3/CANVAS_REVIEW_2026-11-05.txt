IMPLEMENTATION ADDITIONS TO EXPLAIN

These are additions relative to the activity lists inspected, not findings
that the methods are forbidden or absent from every course resource.

1. Camera marker orientation supplies absolute heading, giving a three-value
   observation. A single marker's range/bearing alone has a pose ambiguity.
2. The camera has a base offset; numerical Jacobians account for that offset.
3. Wrapped angular residuals avoid a false jump across -pi/pi. Joseph-form
   covariance updates and symmetrisation help numerical behaviour.
4. NIS gating rejects unusually large innovations. It does not guarantee
   correctness or fix calibration bias. Constant diagonal R is a limitation.
5. Occupancy mapping/A*, route latching, docking confirmation/hysteresis and
   LiDAR stops connect estimation and control into a complete navigation task.
6. Identified actuator feedforward is enabled for path following only in the
   published final configuration. Explain friction/gain, wheel saturation and
   the speed-versus-error tradeoff; this is not a live truth feedback signal.
7. Replay sweeps, NIS/NEES diagnostics, archives and video tools support evidence.
   They are not extra independent physical/navigation trials or proof that the
   students personally ran the experiments.
